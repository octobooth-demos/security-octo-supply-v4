## Copilot Spaces

- **id**: capability.copilot-spaces

Copilot Spaces lets developers organize and centralize relevant content—like repositories, code, pull requests, issues, free-text content like transcripts or notes, images, and file uploads—into “spaces” that ground Copilot’s responses in the right context for a specific task.

### Value Proposal

- **Smarter, Context-Aware AI:** Copilot gives better answers grounded in real project context.
- **Improved Team Productivity:** Spaces help teams stay aligned and reduce redundant questions.
- **Faster Onboarding & Ramp:** New developers get up to speed quickly with curated Spaces.
- **Scalable Knowledge Sharing:** Reduce reliance on tribal knowledge and SME bottlenecks.
- **Accelerated Delivery:** Developers spend less time searching and more time building.
